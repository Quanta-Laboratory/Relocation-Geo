The transit visa — category **T** — is the simplest of Georgia's five visa categories. It exists for one purpose: **transiting Georgia on the way to a third country**. It is defined in Article 7 of the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons.

## What it covers

A transit visa is issued for **transiting the territory of Georgia (up to 10 days)** in order to enter a third country. It is a **short-term** visa. You would use it only if your nationality requires a visa and your trip through Georgia is genuine transit rather than a visit.

## When you do not need it

- If your nationality is on the [visa-free list](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia), you can already enter and stay up to a year — no transit visa needed.
- If you actually intend to spend time in Georgia, the transit visa is the wrong category. Use [visa-free entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia) or the [ordinary (C) visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia) (C1 for tourism).

## How to apply

Eligible nationals apply through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs **e-visa** portal or at a consulate; the portal sets out current eligibility and fees.
