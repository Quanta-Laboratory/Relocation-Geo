The **transit visa (category T)** is the simplest of Georgia's visa categories. It is intended for one purpose only: **transiting through Georgia on the way to a third country**. 

## What it covers

A transit visa is issued to foreign nationals who need to pass through the territory of Georgia in order to travel to a **third country**.

It is a **short-term visa** and permits a stay in Georgia of **up to 10 calendar days**. It is intended only for genuine transit and is not designed for tourism, business or other short-term visits.

## When you do not need a transit visa

You generally **do not** need a transit visa if:

- your nationality qualifies for **[Visa-Free Entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)**; or
- you are otherwise entitled to enter Georgia without a visa under Georgian law.

If you intend to spend time in Georgia rather than simply passing through, the transit visa is not the appropriate category. Instead, you should rely on **[Visa-Free Entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)** (if eligible) or apply for an **[Ordinary (C) Visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia)**, typically **C1** for tourism.

## How to apply

Foreign nationals who are eligible for the **e-Visa** programme may apply online through the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs' e-Visa Portal**, without visiting a Georgian embassy or consulate.

Applicants who are not eligible for an e-Visa should apply through the appropriate **Georgian embassy or consulate**. The Ministry's website provides information on eligibility, documentary requirements and the applicable consular fees.
