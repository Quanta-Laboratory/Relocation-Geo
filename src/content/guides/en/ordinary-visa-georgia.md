The **ordinary visa (category C)** is the standard **short-term Georgian visa** for foreign nationals who require a visa to enter Georgia. It is one of the visa categories established by **Article 7 of the Law of Georgia on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons**.

If your nationality qualifies for **[visa-free entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)**, you will generally **not** need an ordinary visa. Category C is primarily intended for travellers whose nationality requires a Georgian visa.

## The five sub-categories

Georgia issues five types of ordinary visas:

- **C1 – Tourism:** for persons travelling to Georgia for tourism purposes.
- **C2 – Private visits:** for visiting relatives and friends in Georgia.
- **C3 – Business and professional visits:** for business meetings and negotiations, participation in scientific, educational, cultural and sporting events, journalistic assignments, crew members of ships calling at Georgian ports (unless an international treaty provides otherwise), and principal drivers and co-drivers engaged in international passenger or cargo transport.
- **C4 – Humanitarian and other personal purposes:** for humanitarian assistance in life- or health-threatening situations, charitable activities, guardianship, medical treatment, visiting graves of relatives or friends, pilgrimage and other religious visits.
- **C5 – Activities for a non-resident:** for foreign nationals who carry out labour activity or provide services **solely for a non-resident**, where those activities are connected with the non-resident's operations **outside Georgia**. The category also extends to the applicant's spouse and minor children.

## Type and validity

An ordinary visa is a **short-term visa**.

It may be issued as:

- a **single-entry visa** permitting a stay of up to **30 calendar days**; or
- a **multiple-entry visa** with a validity period of **up to five years**.

A **C5 visa** may be issued as a **multiple-entry visa** with a validity period of **up to five years**, allowing its holder to remain in Georgia for **up to one year** on each entry.

## How to apply

Foreign nationals who are eligible for the **e-Visa** programme may apply online through the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs' e-Visa Portal**, without visiting a Georgian embassy or consulate.

The portal also enables applicants to check their eligibility, review the documentary requirements and pay the applicable consular fee online.

## Working on a C visa — the 2026 change

Holding a visa **does not automatically give you the right to work in Georgia**.

Since **1 March 2026**, most foreign nationals who wish to engage in employment, self-employment or entrepreneurial activity in Georgia must first obtain a **Right to Labour Activity**, unless they fall within one of the statutory exemptions.

A **C5 visa** is intended for persons working **solely for a non-resident**, where the activity is connected with that non-resident's operations outside Georgia. Whether a work permit is required depends on the specific activity being carried out. If you intend to work or earn income in Georgia, see the **[Right to Work Guide](/en/right-to-work-georgia)**.

## Planning a longer stay?

An ordinary visa is intended for **temporary visits**.

If you plan to relocate to Georgia for work, study, family reunification, investment or another long-term purpose, you should consider applying for an **[Immigration (D) Visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia)** or, where the law permits, apply directly for the appropriate **[Residence Permit](/en/guide-to-residence-permits)** while lawfully present in Georgia.
