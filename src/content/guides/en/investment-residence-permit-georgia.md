The **Investment Residence Permit** is Georgia's investor route — the closest thing the country has to a "golden visa." It rewards a substantial commitment to the Georgian economy with a comparatively long permit of **up to five years** and a clear path to **[indefinite residence](https://relocation.ge/en/indefinite-residence-permit-georgia/)**. This guide explains the two ways to qualify, the turnover conditions that make the investment route demanding in practice, the fees and the onward path — checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520**.

## Two ways to qualify

There are two qualifying routes, each set at **USD 300,000** (in Georgian lari equivalent):

An **investment** of at least **USD 300,000** in Georgia; or

Ownership of **non-agricultural real estate** with a **market value above USD 300,000**.

In both cases the permit extends to the investor's **family members**. Market value for the property route is established by valuation, not simply the purchase price.

## The turnover schedule (investment route)

The investment route is not a one-off payment that is then forgotten. The associated business must demonstrate rising **annual turnover** across the permit's life:

at least **USD 50,000** in the **first year**;

at least **USD 100,000** in the **second year**; and

at least **USD 120,000** in **each of years three, four and five**.

These are GEL-equivalent figures. **Failing to meet the turnover in a given year can terminate the permit**, so the investment route suits an active, revenue-generating business rather than a passive holding.

## The path to indefinite residence

After **five years**, an investor who has met the turnover conditions — or, on the property route, held the qualifying real estate for the period — can apply for an **[indefinite residence permit](https://relocation.ge/en/indefinite-residence-permit-georgia/)**, which removes the renewal cycle. The fifth-year turnover benchmark of **USD 120,000** is the figure that typically gates that transition for the investment route.

## Investment vs. the property Short-Term permit

It is easy to confuse the two real-estate routes. The **[Short-Term Residence Permit](https://relocation.ge/en/short-term-residence-permit-property-georgia/)** needs non-agricultural property worth more than **USD 150,000** (threshold from 1 March 2026) and is issued **one year at a time**. The Investment Residence Permit needs **USD 300,000** and runs for **up to five years** with the indefinite-residence pathway. Choose the route that matches the value of your holding and the duration you need.

## Fees and timing

Review fees depend on speed: **GEL 300** for a **30-day** decision, **GEL 450** for **20 days**, and **GEL 600** for **10 days**. A **residence (ID) card** is issued separately with its own fee (from GEL 60 for ten working days up to GEL 150 for same-day). Fee schedules change, so confirm the current figures with the **Public Service Development Agency**.

## How to apply

Document the qualifying investment or property ownership and its value, assemble proof of the turnover record where the investment route applies, and file **before any current permit expires**. Respond promptly to any request for further evidence during the review.

This guide is general information, **not legal or investment advice**. Thresholds, turnover conditions and fees can change, so confirm the current position with the **Public Service Development Agency** or an adviser before committing funds.
