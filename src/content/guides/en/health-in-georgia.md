Staying healthy in Georgia has two sides: **getting care** — private clinics, pharmacies and the 112 emergency line — and **being insured**. Since 1 January 2026 the insurance side matters at the border too: tourists must hold health and accident cover of at least GEL 30,000.

## The two sides of health cover

**Everyday care.** Private clinics and pharmacies are accessible and affordable, especially in Tbilisi and Batumi, and 112 is the single emergency number. Most care is paid privately or through insurance rather than the state programme.

**Insurance.** Since 1 January 2026 tourists must carry health and accident insurance of at least GEL 30,000 for their stay. Residents are not covered by the state programme and typically buy private insurance for routine and major care alike.
