People who once held Georgian citizenship and gave it up — frequently because their new country did not allow dual nationality — have a dedicated way back. The **Former Citizen Residence Permit** lets them live in Georgia again on the strength of that history, without having to satisfy the work, investment or family criteria. This guide explains who qualifies, what evidence establishes former citizenship, the duration and fees, and how the permit relates to regaining citizenship — checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520**.

## Who qualifies

The permit is for foreigners who **previously held Georgian citizenship that has since ended** — typically citizenship that was **renounced or terminated**, often on acquiring another nationality. The qualifying fact is the **former citizenship itself**, which is why this route exists separately from the standard permit categories.

## Duration

The permit is issued for **up to six years** and is **renewable** — a notably long term among Georgia's temporary permits, reflecting the settled connection a former citizen has to the country.

## Proving former citizenship

You establish eligibility by **documenting the former citizenship and its termination**. Useful evidence includes **archival citizenship records**, an **old Georgian passport or ID card**, or a **confirmation from the competent authority**, presented together with your **current foreign identity documents**. Foreign documents should be **legalised or apostilled** and **translated** where required.

## A residence permit, not citizenship

It is worth being clear: this is a **residence permit**, not a restoration of citizenship. **Reacquiring Georgian citizenship** is a separate process under the citizenship rules. The former-citizen permit gives you a **lawful, long-term basis to live in Georgia** while you decide on — or pursue — any citizenship route. Time spent lawfully on qualifying temporary permits can also count toward the **ten years** for a **[permanent residence permit](https://relocation.ge/en/permanent-residence-permit-georgia/)**.

## Fees and how to apply

Review fees depend on speed: **GEL 300** for a **30-day** decision, **GEL 450** for **20 days**, and **GEL 600** for **10 days**, plus a separate **residence-card** fee. Assemble proof of your former citizenship and current identity, and file **before any current permit expires**. Confirm current fees and the document list with the **Public Service Development Agency**.

This guide is general information, **not legal advice**. Eligibility evidence and fees can change, so confirm the current position with the **Public Service Development Agency** or an adviser before relying on a particular treatment.
