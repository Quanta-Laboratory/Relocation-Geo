A **work residence permit** is a temporary residence permit granted to a **foreign national who carries out labour or entrepreneurial activity in Georgia** . In most cases, applicants must first obtain the **right to work in Georgia**, although Georgian legislation provides  exceptions for certain categories of foreign nationals whose activities fall outside the scope of the **Law on Labour Migration**. 
Eligible family members may also apply for a **[Family Reunification Residence Permit](/en/family-reunification-residence-permit-georgia)**.

## Who is eligible

A foreigner may obtain a **work residence permit** if they carry out **labour or entrepreneurial activity in Georgia**. 

Applicants generally fall into one of the following categories:

**A. Employment.** The foreign national is **employed by an employer registered in Georgia** and carries out labour activity under an employment relationship. The employment must be supported by an employment contract or another document confirming employment.

**B. Self-employment / entrepreneurial activity.** The foreign national carries out **entrepreneurial activity in Georgia**, including:
- registration as an **[Individual Entrepreneur (IE)](/en/register-individual-entrepreneur-remotely)** in Georgia;
- participation as a **partner or shareholder in a Georgian company**, with involvement in its activities;
- other forms of **lawful entrepreneurial activity** recognised under Georgian legislation.

## Financial requirements 

Applicants for a Work Residence Permit must generally satisfy both **income** and **business turnover** requirements.
### Minimum monthly income

The applicant must demonstrate a **monthly income or remuneration** from labour or entrepreneurial activity of **at least five times the average consumer subsistence minimum** established in Georgia.

If the employment or business documents do not sufficiently demonstrate the applicant's legal income, **funds held in the applicant's personal bank account** may also be taken into account, provided the available amount corresponds to at least five times the average consumer subsistence minimum for each month of the requested permit period.

### Employer or business turnover

The employer, or the enterprise established by the applicant, must generally demonstrate an **annual turnover of at least GEL 50,000 per foreign applicant**.

For **educational and medical institutions**, the required annual turnover is **at least GEL 35,000 per foreign applicant**.

## Required documents

Applications are submitted to the **Public Service Development Agency of Georgia**. 

### A. Employees
Applicants employed by an employer registered in Georgia generally submit:

- a completed **application form**, including the **unique code assigned by the State Employment Promotion Agency**;
- a **copy of the passport**;
- **proof of lawful stay** in Georgia;
- a document confirming the **employment relationship**, such as an employment contract or another document confirming employment;
- the **decision granting the right to work** in Georgia;
- a document confirming that the applicant's **monthly remuneration** is at least **five times the average consumer subsistence minimum** established in Georgia (or, where the employment documents do not sufficiently demonstrate legal income, evidence of sufficient funds held in the applicant's personal bank account);
- a document issued by the **Revenue Service** confirming that the employer's annual turnover is at least **GEL 50,000 per foreign applicant**, or **GEL 35,000** for educational or medical institutions;
- a certificate confirming the **number of foreign nationals employed** by the employer;
- a **3×4 colour photograph** in electronic format;
- the **receipt confirming payment** of the state fee.

### B. Individual Entrepreneurs

Applicants carrying out entrepreneurial activity generally submit:

- a completed **application form**, including the **unique code assigned by the State Employment Promotion Agency**;
- a **copy of the passport**;
- **proof of lawful stay** in Georgia;
- documents confirming the **entrepreneurial activity** carried out in Georgia;
- the **decision granting the right to work** in Georgia;
- a document confirming that the applicant's **monthly income** from entrepreneurial activity is at least **five times the average consumer subsistence minimum** established in Georgia (or, where necessary, evidence of sufficient funds held in the applicant's personal bank account);
- a document issued by the **Revenue Service** confirming that the enterprise's annual turnover is at least **GEL 50,000 per foreign applicant**, or **GEL 35,000** for educational or medical institutions;
- a certificate confirming the **number of foreign nationals** employed by the enterprise;
- a **3×4 colour photograph** in electronic format;
- the **receipt confirming payment** of the state fee.

### Additional documents in specific cases

Depending on the applicant's circumstances, additional or alternative documents may be required:

- **Applicants carrying out activities exempt from the Law on Labour Migration** must submit documents confirming the relevant exempt activity.
- Where the employer or enterprise is **not registered as a VAT payer**, alternative certificates confirming the required turnover and non-VAT status may be submitted instead of the Revenue Service document.
- If the required turnover certificates **cannot objectively be obtained**, other reliable evidence demonstrating compliance with the turnover requirement may be accepted.
- **Newly established enterprises** (registered for no more than three months, employing no more than three foreign nationals and applying for a first Work Residence Permit) are exempt from the standard turnover documentation. Instead, they submit a certificate confirming the number of foreign employees. In such cases, the first Work Residence Permit is issued for **six months**.

Documents issued outside Georgia must generally be legalised or apostilled, unless an applicable international treaty provides otherwise. Documents in a foreign language must be translated into Georgian and notarised. Where permitted by law, foreign documents may also be submitted in electronic form without the original, provided the authenticity of the apostille or legalisation can be verified electronically.
## Duration

A work residence permit is usually issued for **6 months to 1 year**, Depending on the applicant's circumstances. It may be **extended**, provided the foreign national continues to meet the statutory requirements for the permit.

One important exception applies to **newly established enterprises**. Where an applicant benefits from the **turnover exemption** for companies registered within the previous three months, employing no more than three foreign nationals and applying for a first Work Residence Permit, the initial permit is issued for **six months**. Before applying for an extension, the enterprise will generally be expected to satisfy the standard turnover requirements.

## Processing time and state fees

The state fee depends on how quickly the application is processed:

| Processing time | State fee |
| --- | --- |
| 30 calendar days | GEL 300 |
| 20 calendar days | GEL 450 |
| 10 calendar days | GEL 600 |

## Georgian ID (residence) card

After obtaining the residence permit, a foreign national must apply for a  **Georgian residence (ID) card** within one month. Applications may be submitted through the Public Service Hall or a territorial office of the Public Service Development Agency.

The fee depends on the processing time:

| Issuance time | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| 10 working days | GEL 60 |
| 5 working days | GEL 80 |
| 3 working days | GEL 100 |
| 2 working days | GEL 120 |
| Same day | GEL 150 |

## Family members

**Eligible family members** of a foreign national holding a Work Residence Permit may also apply for a **Family Reunification Residence Permit**. The categories of eligible family members and the applicable documentary requirements are explained in the **[family reunification residence permit](/en/family-reunification-residence-permit-georgia)** guide.
