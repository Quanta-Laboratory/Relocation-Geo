The diplomatic visa — category **A** — is a short-term Georgian visa reserved for official and diplomatic travel. It is one of the five visa categories set out in Article 7 of the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons. If you are relocating, working remotely or visiting as a tourist, this is **not** your visa; see [visa-free entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia) or the [ordinary (C) visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia) instead. This page is here for completeness and for those handling official visits.

## The five subcategories

- **A1** — top officials of foreign legislative and executive authorities, members of top- and high-level delegations, and their accompanying family members, arriving on state, official, business, friendly or unofficial visits. **A1 holders enjoy diplomatic immunity** for the visa's validity.
- **A2** — employees of diplomatic and equivalent missions accredited to Georgia, consular officials and their family members; staff of international-organisation representative offices in Georgia who hold diplomatic status, and their families.
- **A3** — diplomatic couriers and individuals arriving on a special diplomatic mission.
- **A4** — honorary consuls of Georgia who are citizens of a foreign country, and their family members.
- **A5** — in the national interests of Georgia, individuals arriving on various missions, or holders of diplomatic passports arriving on a working visit.

## Type and validity

A diplomatic visa is a **short-term** visa. A single-entry short-term visa is issued for up to 30 days; multiple-entry short-term visas can be issued with validity of up to five years. Where a longer stay tied to accreditation is needed, note that a long-term visa is the precondition for obtaining diplomatic accreditation or a residence permit.

## Extension

Short-term diplomatic (and special) visas can be **extended** if the conditions for issuing the respective visa are still met. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia receives the documentation and makes the decision.

## Not the right category?

Category A is narrow and official. Most people arriving in Georgia use one of these instead: [visa-free entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia) for listed nationalities, the [ordinary (C) visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia) for tourism, business or remote work, or an [immigration (D) visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia) as the first step toward a residence permit.
