The **diplomatic visa (category A)** is a **short-term Georgian visa** intended exclusively for diplomatic and official travel. It is one of the visa categories established by **Article 7 of the Law of Georgia on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons**.

If you are travelling to Georgia for tourism, business, remote work or relocation, this is **not** the appropriate visa category. Instead, see **[Visa-Free Entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)**, the **[Ordinary (C) Visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia)** or the **[Immigration (D) Visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia)**.

## The five sub-categories

Georgia issues five types of diplomatic visas:

- **A1** — for senior officials of foreign legislative and executive authorities, members of high-level state delegations and their accompanying family members travelling on state, official, working, friendly or unofficial visits.
- **A2** — for members of diplomatic missions and equivalent representative offices accredited to Georgia, consular officers and their family members, as well as staff of international organisations' representative offices in Georgia who enjoy diplomatic status and their family members.
- **A3** — for diplomatic couriers and persons travelling to Georgia on a special diplomatic mission.
- **A4** — for honorary consuls of Georgia who are foreign nationals and their accompanying family members.
- **A5** — for persons whose entry serves the national interests of Georgia, as well as holders of diplomatic passports travelling on an official working visit.

## Type and validity

A diplomatic visa is a **short-term visa**.

It may be issued as:

- a **single-entry visa** permitting a stay of up to **30 calendar days**; or
- a **multiple-entry visa** with a validity period of **up to five years**, in accordance with the Law.

The type of visa issued depends on the purpose and circumstances of the official visit.

## Extension

A diplomatic visa may be **extended** where the legal grounds for its issuance continue to exist.

Applications for an extension are submitted through the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia**, which considers the application and makes the relevant decision in accordance with the Law.

## Is this the right visa for you?

For the vast majority of travellers, the answer is **no**.

Most visitors to Georgia instead rely on:

- **[Visa-Free Entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)**, if their nationality qualifies;
- the **[Ordinary (C) Visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia)** for tourism, private visits and most short-term business travel; or
- the **[Immigration (D) Visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia)** for long-term purposes such as employment, study, family reunification or investment.
