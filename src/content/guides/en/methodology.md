Relocation.ge follows an open-standard approach to turning official legal provisions into clear, usable guidance. For what the project is and why it exists, see [About Relocation.ge](/en/about).

## Our principles

- **Structured legal definitions** — each page is based on a specific legal source, cited and linked.
- **Two-date transparency** — every page shows when it was last reviewed by a human and when it was last automatically checked.
- **Version-controlled updates** — content history is tracked, so changes are auditable.
- **Clear scope** — guidance explains the law as published; it is not legal advice.

## Sources

Information is structured from official publications, including Matsne (Legislative Herald), the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Revenue Service, and the Public Service Hall.
