The **Indefinite Residence Permit** is where the **investor route** leads. A foreigner who has carried an **[Investment Residence Permit](/en/investment-residence-permit-georgia)** through its full five years — and met the conditions — can convert it into a permit with the **right to reside in Georgia indefinitely**, with **no expiry and no renewal cycle**. Family members of the investment-permit holder may also obtain an Indefinite Residence Permit. This guide sets out who qualifies, the documents required, the timing that matters and the fees, checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520 of 1 September 2014**.

## Who is eligible

An Indefinite Residence Permit may be granted to a foreigner who has held an **Investment Residence Permit in Georgia for five years**, where that permit was obtained on the basis of either:

- an **investment of at least USD 300,000** (or its equivalent in GEL); or
- **ownership of immovable property in Georgia valued at more than USD 300,000**.

The application must be submitted **before the expiration of the five-year Investment Residence Permit**.

## Duration and validity

Once granted, the Indefinite Residence Permit gives the holder the **right to reside in Georgia indefinitely**. Unlike the temporary permits, it is **not tied to a renewal cycle** and does not carry a fixed expiry for the right of residence. The Georgian ID (residence) card issued on its basis is renewed periodically as a document, but the underlying status is indefinite.

## Application

Applications are submitted to the **Public Service Development Agency of Georgia**.

## Required documents

The document set depends on how the underlying Investment Residence Permit was obtained.

**Investment route (USD 300,000 investment):**

- the **application**;
- a **copy of the passport** or travel document;
- a **copy of the Investment Residence Permit / residence card** issued on the basis of a USD 300,000 investment;
- **information on the fifth-year turnover** — confirmation that annual turnover from entrepreneurial activity in Georgia in the **fifth year** after the investment permit was issued was **not less than the GEL equivalent of USD 120,000**;
- a **colour photograph, 3×4**, in electronic format;
- the **receipt** confirming payment of the service fee.

**Real-estate route (property valued above USD 300,000):**

- the **application**;
- a **copy of the passport** or travel document;
- a **copy of the Investment Residence Permit / residence card** issued on the basis of ownership of real estate valued at more than USD 300,000, together with a **certificate from the National Agency of Public Registry** confirming ownership of the relevant property (or of lawfully substituted property) for a **total period of five years**;
- a **colour photograph, 3×4**, in electronic format;
- the **receipt** confirming payment of the service fee.

**Family members** applying for an Indefinite Residence Permit submit:

- the **application**;
- a **copy of the passport** or travel document;
- a **document confirming lawful stay** in Georgia;
- a **copy of the Investment Residence Permit** of the main applicant;
- a **document confirming the family relationship**;
- a **colour photograph, 3×4**, in electronic format;
- the **receipt** confirming payment of the service fee.

Foreign documents must generally be **legalised or apostilled** and translated into Georgian and notarised, unless an exemption applies.

## Timing: apply before expiry

The application must be filed **before the Investment Residence Permit expires**. As a procedural safeguard, where a holder of an Investment Residence Permit applies for an Indefinite Residence Permit, the **investment permit remains valid until the Agency makes its decision** on the indefinite application. Even so, allowing the underlying permit to lapse before applying can break the chain, so prepare the application well ahead of the five-year mark.

## Indefinite vs. permanent

Georgia's two indefinite-stay permits are reached by different routes. The **Indefinite Residence Permit** here is the **investor** path — five years on an investment permit. The **[Permanent Residence Permit](/en/permanent-residence-permit-georgia)** is for **close family members of Georgian citizens** or for those with **ten years of lawful residence**. Both end the renewal cycle; choose the description that matches how you qualified. For an overview of all categories, see the **[guide to residence permits](/en/guide-to-residence-permits)**.

## Processing time and state fees

The state fee depends on how quickly the application is processed:

| Processing time | State fee |
| --- | --- |
| 30 calendar days | GEL 350 |
| 20 calendar days | GEL 500 |

## Georgian ID (residence) card

After obtaining the residence permit, a foreign national may apply for a **Georgian ID (residence) card** through the Public Service Hall or the Public Registry. The fee depends on the processing time:

| Issuance time | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| 10 working days | GEL 60 |
| 5 working days | GEL 80 |
| 3 working days | GEL 100 |
| 2 working days | GEL 120 |
| Same day | GEL 150 |

## Legal sources

This guide is based on the **Law of Georgia on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government of Georgia Resolution No. 520 of 1 September 2014** on the procedure for reviewing and deciding on the issuance of residence permits in Georgia. Official versions are published in the **Legislative Herald of Georgia (Matsne)**. Thresholds, fees and processing times are periodically updated, so confirm the current requirements with the **Public Service Development Agency** before applying.
