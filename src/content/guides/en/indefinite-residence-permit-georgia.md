The **Indefinite Residence Permit** is where the **investor route** leads. A foreigner who has carried an **[Investment Residence Permit](https://relocation.ge/en/investment-residence-permit-georgia/)** through its full five years — and met the conditions — can convert it into a permit with **no expiry and no renewal cycle**. This guide explains who qualifies, the fifth-year conditions, the timing that matters and how the indefinite permit differs from the permanent permit, checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520**.

## Who qualifies

The indefinite permit is available to a foreigner (and their **family members**) who:

held an **Investment Residence Permit for five years** on the basis of a **USD 300,000** investment, and shows **annual turnover of at least USD 120,000** in the **fifth year**; or

held that investment permit on the **real-estate route** and has **owned the qualifying non-agricultural property for the full five years**.

## The fifth-year condition

On the investment route, the gateway figure is the **fifth-year turnover of at least USD 120,000** (in GEL equivalent). This sits at the top of the Investment Residence Permit's rising turnover schedule — **USD 50,000** in year one, **USD 100,000** in year two, and **USD 120,000** in years three to five — so an investor who has kept the business above those benchmarks throughout is well placed to step up to indefinite status. On the property route, the equivalent test is **continued ownership** of the qualifying real estate across the period.

## Timing: apply before expiry

The single most important practical point is **timing**: the indefinite application must be filed **before the Investment Residence Permit expires**. Allowing the underlying permit to lapse can break the chain and force you to start over, so prepare the indefinite application well ahead of the five-year mark.

## Indefinite vs. permanent

Georgia's two indefinite-stay permits are reached by different routes. The **Indefinite Residence Permit** here is the **investor** path — five years on an investment permit. The **[Permanent Residence Permit](https://relocation.ge/en/permanent-residence-permit-georgia/)** is for **close family members of Georgian citizens** or for those with **ten years of lawful residence**. Both end the renewal cycle; choose the description that matches how you qualified.

## Fees and how to apply

Review fees are **GEL 350** for a **30-day** decision and **GEL 500** for a **20-day** decision, plus a separate **residence-card** fee depending on speed of issue. Assemble proof of the five-year investment permit and the fifth-year turnover (or the five-year property ownership), and file **before the investment permit expires**. Confirm current fees and the document list with the **Public Service Development Agency**.

This guide is general information, **not legal or investment advice**. Turnover conditions, timing rules and fees can change, so confirm the current position with the **Public Service Development Agency** or an adviser before relying on a particular treatment.
