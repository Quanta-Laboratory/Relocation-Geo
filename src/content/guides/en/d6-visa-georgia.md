The **D6** visa is a new sub-category of Georgia's immigration (**D**) visa, added to the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons by 2026 amendments and **coming into force on 1 September 2026**. It is built specifically around **minor foreign nationals who come to Georgia to study**, and — importantly — it extends to the family accompanying them. It is defined in Article 7 of the Law, alongside the other visa categories.

## Who D6 is for

The category is issued to:

- a **minor** coming to or already in Georgia to study at an **authorised higher or vocational education institution**;
- a **minor** coming to or already in Georgia to study at an **authorised general-education school**, up until they **complete full general education**;
- where the foreign student is a minor, **their parents or legal representatives and their minor siblings** — until the student **reaches the age of majority (18)**.

In other words, D6 keeps a school-age or student minor and their immediate family on the same long-term footing, rather than requiring the accompanying parents to qualify separately.

## Type, validity and stay

D6 is a **long-term (immigration) visa**. Like all D visas it is **multiple-entry**, issued with **90-day or one-year** validity and duration of stay. Under the amended rule that takes effect on 1 September 2026, the **one-year** long-term visa can be issued for **only two** categories: **D5** (property owners) and the new **D6**. As with every immigration visa, a D6 is the **precondition for obtaining a Georgian residence permit**.

## Effective date and the D3 reform

D6 comes into force on **1 September 2026**. It arrives as part of a paired change: from the same date, the **D3** study-and-research category is narrowed to **adult** students and researchers. The effect is a clean split — adults who study or do research use **D3**, while **minors** who study use **D6**, and D6 uniquely brings the accompanying parents or legal representatives and minor siblings with them.

## From D6 to a residence permit

Because it is a long-term visa, D6 is the entry step rather than the destination. Living in Georgia beyond the visa means converting it into a [residence permit](/en/guide-to-residence-permits); for study, the relevant permits sit within Georgia's education-based residence framework. See the [immigration (D) visa guide](/en/immigration-visa-georgia) for how the D categories relate and the general processing timeline.

## How to apply

Eligible applicants apply through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs **e-visa** portal or at a Georgian consulate; the portal sets out current eligibility, documents and fees. As this category only takes effect on **1 September 2026**, confirm that the application channel and required documents are live before relying on it.
