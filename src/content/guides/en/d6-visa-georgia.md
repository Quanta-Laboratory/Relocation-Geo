The **D6 visa** is a new sub-category of Georgia's **immigration (D) visa**, introduced by the 2026 amendments to the **Law of Georgia on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons**. It takes effect on **1 September 2026**.

The category is designed specifically for **minor foreign nationals who come to Georgia for education** and, importantly, also covers the family members accompanying them.

## Who the D6 visa is for

The D6 visa may be issued to:

- a **minor** who comes to, or is already lawfully present in, Georgia to study at an **authorised higher education institution** or **authorised vocational education institution**;
- a **minor** who comes to, or is already lawfully present in, Georgia to study at an **authorised general education institution**, until completing full general education;
- where the student is a minor, the student's **parents or other legal representatives** and **minor siblings**, until the student reaches **18 years of age**.

The purpose of the category is to allow a minor student and the accompanying immediate family to remain in Georgia under the same immigration framework.

## Type and validity

The D6 visa is a **long-term (immigration) visa**.

Like all D visas, it is a **multiple-entry visa** that may be issued with a validity period of **90 calendar days** or **one year**, depending on the applicable category.

From **1 September 2026**, a **one-year D visa** may be issued only under **D5** (certain property owners) and **D6**.

A D6 visa is intended for foreign nationals relocating to Georgia for long-term educational purposes and may also serve as the basis for applying for a **Georgian residence permit**, where the statutory requirements are met.

## Effective date and the D3 reform

The D6 category takes effect on **1 September 2026**.

At the same time, the scope of the **D3** visa is narrowed to **adult** students and researchers. As a result:

- **adult students and researchers** use **D3**;
- **minor students** use **D6**; and
- the **parents or other legal representatives** and **minor siblings** accompanying a minor student may also obtain a **D6** visa.

## Residence permits

A D6 visa allows eligible applicants to enter and remain in Georgia for long-term educational purposes.

If the student and accompanying family intend to continue living in Georgia beyond the period authorised by the visa, they may apply for the appropriate **[Residence Permit](/en/guide-to-residence-permits)**, provided the statutory requirements are met. For an overview of the D-visa system, see the **[Immigration (D) Visa Guide](/en/immigration-visa-georgia)**.

## How to apply

Applications for a **D6 visa** are submitted through the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia** and the relevant **Georgian embassy or consulate**, in accordance with the applicable visa procedures.

As the D6 category takes effect only on **1 September 2026**, applicants should check the latest official guidance on the application procedure, required documents and consular fees before applying.
