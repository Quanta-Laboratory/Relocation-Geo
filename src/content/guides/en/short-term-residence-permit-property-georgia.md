For many foreigners the simplest way to live in Georgia is to **own property there**. The **Short-Term Residence Permit** turns qualifying real-estate ownership into a residence right, renewable year by year for as long as you hold the property. The headline number changed in 2026, so the threshold and the transitional rule are the first things to get right. Everything below is checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520**.

## The threshold: USD 150,000 from 1 March 2026

To qualify you must own **non-agricultural real estate** in Georgia with a **market value above USD 150,000** (in Georgian lari equivalent). This threshold took effect on **1 March 2026**, raising the previous figure of **USD 100,000** by half.

A crucial **transitional rule** protects earlier buyers: if your **ownership was registered before 1 March 2026**, the **old USD 100,000 threshold** still applies — even if you file the residence-permit application after that date. The higher figure bites only on ownership registered on or after 1 March 2026.

## Value is by valuation, not price

The decisive figure is the **market value set by an accredited assessor**, not the price on your contract. If the appraisal comes in **below the threshold**, the property cannot support the permit. You may **combine several properties** to reach the figure, provided each is properly registered in the **Public Registry** and valued. See the [buying property guide](https://relocation.ge/en/buying-property-in-georgia-for-foreigners/) for how ownership and registration work.

## Duration and family

The permit is **short-term** — issued for **one year at a time** and **renewable** for as long as you continue to own qualifying property above the threshold. It can extend to the owner's **spouse and minor children**, so one qualifying property can cover the immediate family.

## How it differs from the Investment permit

Do not confuse this with the **[Investment Residence Permit](https://relocation.ge/en/investment-residence-permit-georgia/)**. The property Short-Term permit needs real estate above **USD 150,000** and is renewed **yearly**. The Investment permit needs **USD 300,000** (as investment or property), runs **up to five years** and leads to **indefinite residence**. If your holding sits between the two thresholds, the Short-Term route is the one available to you.

## Fees and how to apply

A **residence (ID) card** is issued on approval, with a fee depending on speed of issue (from GEL 60 for ten working days up to GEL 150 for same-day). Document your **ownership and a current valuation** above the threshold, file **before any current permit expires**, and respond promptly to any request for further evidence. Confirm current fees and the documents required with the **Public Service Development Agency**.

This guide is general information, **not legal advice**. Thresholds, the transitional rule and fees can change, so confirm the current position with the **Public Service Development Agency** or an adviser before relying on a particular treatment.
