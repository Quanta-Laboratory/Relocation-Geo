Georgia allows a foreign national to register an **Individual Entrepreneur (IE)** **in person** through a fast procedure at the **Public Service Hall**. This guide sets out the step-by-step process for registering an IE when you are physically present in the country.

In-person registration is commonly used by freelancers, IT specialists, consultants and other remote service providers. See also the guides on [running a business in Georgia](/en/run-a-business-in-georgia) and [business statuses in Georgia](/en/business-statuses-georgia). If you cannot travel, an IE can also be [registered remotely](/en/register-individual-entrepreneur-remotely) through a Power of Attorney.

## Who can register in person

To register in person you must be **physically located in Georgia**. You can apply regardless of the basis of your stay, including as a:

- visa-free visitor;
- long-stay (D) visa holder;
- residence permit holder.

## Required documents

You need only a minimal set of documents:

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Passport (original) | Presented at the counter |
| Georgian mobile number | Used for SMS verification during registration |
| Legal address in Georgia | A mandatory registration detail (see [legal address for a company in Georgia](/en/legal-address-for-companies-georgia)) |

Because a **Georgian phone number** is needed to confirm registration by SMS, obtain a local SIM or eSIM before you go.

## Step-by-step process

| Step | Action | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm you are physically present in Georgia | — |
| 2 | Prepare your passport, Georgian phone number and legal address | — |
| 3 | Obtain a Georgian phone number or eSIM for SMS verification | — |
| 4 | Submit your passport and personal details at the counter | Public Service Hall / Public Registry |
| 5 | Register a legal address and pay any applicable fee | Public Service Hall |
| 6 | Receive the official business extract (Georgian and English) | Public Service Hall |
| 7 | Your data is linked to the Revenue Service | Revenue Service |

The process is handled on-site by an operator and usually takes **one working day**.

## The registration extract

After registration you receive an **official business extract**, issued electronically in **Georgian and English**. This document confirms your IE registration and its identification number.

## Registration with the Revenue Service

Once the IE is registered, your data is automatically linked to the **Revenue Service of Georgia**. You can then activate your online account, manage taxes and submit declarations through the Revenue Service portal.

## Small Business status

Most foreign entrepreneurs then apply for **Small Business status** through the **Revenue Service of Georgia**, if they qualify. This regime applies a **1% tax on turnover up to GEL 500,000 per year**, subject to the conditions in the Tax Code. See [personal income tax in Georgia](/en/personal-income-tax-georgia) for the qualifying conditions.

## Right to work

Since 1 March 2026, carrying out entrepreneurial activity in Georgia may also require a separate **right to work** — see the [right to work in Georgia](/en/right-to-work-georgia) guide before you start earning income here.

## After registration

Once registration and tax status are active, you can [open a business bank account](/en/banking-in-georgia), sign contracts, issue invoices, receive payments and work with clients.

Registration procedures, fees and timeframes are periodically updated, so confirm current requirements with the **Public Service Hall**, the **National Agency of Public Registry** or the **Revenue Service** before applying.
