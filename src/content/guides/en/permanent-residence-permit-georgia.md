The **Permanent Residence Permit** is the goal for many long-term residents: the right to live in Georgia **indefinitely**, without the recurring renewals that temporary permits demand. It is reached by two main routes — **family ties to a Georgian citizen** or **ten years of lawful residence**. This guide explains both, what counts toward the ten years, the fees and how the permanent permit sits beside the investment-based indefinite permit — checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520**.

## Route one: family of a Georgian citizen

A foreigner who is a **close family member of a Georgian citizen** can obtain permanent residence through a simplified route rather than waiting out the ten-year period. The qualifying relationships are:

a **spouse** of a Georgian citizen;

a **minor child** of a Georgian citizen; and

a **parent of a minor child** who holds Georgian citizenship.

## Route two: ten years of lawful residence

The other route is **ten years of continuous lawful residence** in Georgia on **temporary residence permits**. The important qualification is that **not every permit counts**: time spent on a **study residence permit** or on a permit issued for **medical treatment** is generally **excluded** from the ten-year count. Because the count is cumulative and document-driven, keep your permits, entry/exit records and address history so you can evidence the full period.

## What the permit does — and does not — give you

Permanent residence is granted for an **indefinite period** and removes the renewal cycle, putting the holder on a long-term footing close to that of a citizen for the purpose of living in the country. But it is **not citizenship**: there is no Georgian passport or voting right, and the status can still be **lost** in defined circumstances, such as prolonged absence or other grounds set out in the law. You continue to hold and replace the **physical residence (ID) card** on its own validity.

## Permanent vs. indefinite

Georgia has two indefinite-stay permits and they are reached differently. The **Permanent Residence Permit** here is for **family members of citizens** or **ten-year residents**. The **[Indefinite Residence Permit](https://relocation.ge/en/indefinite-residence-permit-georgia/)** is the onward step for holders of an **[investment residence permit](https://relocation.ge/en/investment-residence-permit-georgia/)** who have met the turnover or property conditions over five years. Both end the renewal treadmill; the qualifying path differs.

## Fees and how to apply

Review fees are **GEL 350** for a **30-day** decision and **GEL 500** for a **20-day** decision, plus a separate **residence-card** fee depending on speed of issue. Establish your qualifying basis — proof of the family relationship and the citizen's status, or evidence of the ten qualifying years — and file **before any current permit expires**. Confirm current fees and document lists with the **Public Service Development Agency**.

This guide is general information, **not legal advice**. The counting rules, grounds for loss and fees can change, so confirm the current position with the **Public Service Development Agency** or an adviser before relying on a particular treatment.
