  - q: Does all my time on temporary permits count toward the ten years?
    a: The ten-year route requires continuous lawful residence on the basis of a temporary residence permit over the last ten years. Keep copies of your residence permits and residence cards, along with your presence and address records, to evidence the full period.

The **Permanent Residence Permit** is the goal for many long-term residents: the right to live in Georgia **indefinitely**, without the recurring renewals required for temporary residence permits. Under Georgian law, it is available only in specific circumstances, principally through **family relationships with a Georgian citizen** or **ten years of lawful residence** in Georgia. 

## Who is eligible

Under Georgian law, a **Permanent Residence Permit** may be granted through **two routes**.

### A. Family members of a Georgian citizen

A Permanent Residence Permit may be granted to:

- the **spouse of a Georgian citizen**;
- the **minor child of a Georgian citizen**; and
- the **parent of a minor Georgian citizen**.

### B. Long-term residents of Georgia

A Permanent Residence Permit may also be granted to a foreign national who has **lawfully lived in Georgia for the last ten years on the basis of a temporary residence permit**.

For this purpose, the ten-year period **does not include** time spent in Georgia:
- for **study**;
- for **medical treatment**; or
- while working at a **diplomatic mission or an equivalent mission**.


## Required documents

Applications for a **Permanent Residence Permit** are submitted to the **Public Service Development Agency (PSDA)**. Depending on the circumstances, an application may be filed **in person**, **through an authorised representative**, or **electronically**, where permitted by law. The required supporting documents depend on the route under which the applicant qualifies.

**Family members of a Georgian citizen:**

- the **application**;
- a **copy of the foreigner's passport**;
- a copy of a document confirming **lawful stay in Georgia**;
- a document confirming the **family relationship** with the Georgian citizen;
- **A document confirming the lawful income in Georgia of the foreign national and/or their relative who is either a Georgian citizen or holds the right of residence in Georgia.** If the Georgian citizen is of **pensionable age** or receives **state social assistance**, a document confirming receipt of the **state pension** or **social assistance** must be submitted. Funds held in the foreign national's **personal bank account** may also be regarded as income.
- a **colour photograph, 3×4** cm, in electronic format;
- the **receipt** confirming payment of the service fee.

**Foreign national applying for a Permanent Residence Permit on the basis of having lived in Georgia for the last 10 years under a temporary residence permit:**

- the **application**;
- a **copy of the foreigner's passport**;
- a document confirming **residence in Georgia for the last ten years** on the basis of a temporary residence permit (copy of the residence permit / residence card);
- a document confirming that the foreign national has **lived in Georgia for the last 10 years on the basis of a temporary residence permit** (a copy of the **temporary residence card** and/or **temporary residence permit**);
- a document confirming the **lawful income in Georgia** of the foreign national and/or their relative who is either a **Georgian citizen** or **holds the right of residence in Georgia**, together with a document confirming the **family relationship**. If the Georgian citizen is of **pensionable age** or receives **state social assistance**, a document confirming receipt of the **state pension** or **social assistance** must instead be submitted. Funds held in the foreign national's **personal bank account** may also be regarded as income.
- a **colour photograph, 3×4** cm, in electronic format;
- the **receipt** confirming payment of the service fee.

## What the permit does — and does not — provide

A **Permanent Residence Permit** allows its holder to **live in Georgia indefinitely**, without the need to renew their residence permit at regular intervals.

However, **permanent residence is not the same as Georgian citizenship**. A Permanent Residence Permit does **not**entitle its holder to a **Georgian passport**, the **right to vote**, or any other rights reserved exclusively to Georgian citizens.

Permanent residence may also be **terminated** in the circumstances prescribed by law, including certain periods of absence from Georgia or other statutory grounds.

Although the residence permit itself is granted for an **indefinite period**, the holder must continue to obtain and renew the **residence card (ID card)** in accordance with the applicable rules.

## Permanent Residence Permit vs. Indefinite Residence Permit

Georgia provides for two residence permits that allow a foreign national to reside in the country **without a fixed expiry date**, but they are intended for different categories of applicants.

The **Permanent Residence Permit** is available primarily to:

- certain **family members of Georgian citizens**; and
- foreign nationals who have **lawfully lived in Georgia for the last ten years on the basis of a temporary residence permit**.

By contrast, the **[Indefinite Residence Permit](/en/indefinite-residence-permit-georgia)** is available to qualifying holders of an **[Investment Residence Permit](/en/investment-residence-permit-georgia)** who continue to satisfy the statutory investment requirements over the prescribed period.

Both permits provide an indefinite right of residence, but the **eligibility criteria and qualifying route differ**.

For an overview of all Georgian residence permits, see our **[Guide to Residence Permits](/en/guide-to-residence-permits)**.

## Processing time and state fees

The state fee for issuing a **Permanent Residence Permit** depends on the selected processing time:

| Processing time | State fee |
| --- | --- |
| 30 calendar days | GEL 350 |
| 20 calendar days | GEL 500 |

Before applying, make sure you can demonstrate the qualifying basis for the permit—either the required **family relationship and legal income**, or **ten years of lawful residence in Georgia** under a temporary residence permit.

If you are applying from within Georgia, ensure that you **remain lawfully in the country** throughout the application process.

As state fees, document requirements and administrative procedures may change, always confirm the current requirements with the **Public Service Development Agency (PSDA)** before submitting your application.

## Georgian residence card

Once a residence permit has been granted, the foreign national must obtain a **Georgian residence card**, which serves as the holder's identity document in Georgia.

Applications for a residence card may be submitted at a **Public Service Hall** or a **territorial office of the Public Service Development Agency (PSDA)**.

The state fee depends on the selected processing time:

| Issuance time | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| 10 working days | GEL 60 |
| 5 working days | GEL 80 |
| 3 working days | GEL 100 |
| 2 working days | GEL 120 |
| Same day | GEL 150 |

A residence card is issued separately from the residence permit decision. Although a **Permanent Residence Permit** is granted for an indefinite period, the **residence card** itself must be renewed or replaced in the cases provided for by law, such as expiry, loss, damage or changes to the holder's personal details.
