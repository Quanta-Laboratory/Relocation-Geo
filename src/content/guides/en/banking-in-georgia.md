Foreign nationals may be able to open both **personal** and **business** bank accounts in Georgia. The country's largest retail banks include **Bank of Georgia**, **TBC Bank** and **Liberty Bank**, although each institution applies its own onboarding procedures and compliance policies.

If you register as an **Individual Entrepreneur (IE)**, opening a business account is often the next practical step. Banks will usually request your **personal number** and, where applicable, your **tax identification number (TIN)**.

## Typical requirements

Although requirements vary between banks, applicants are commonly asked to provide:

- a valid passport;
    
- proof of address (which may be from your home country);
    
- your Georgian personal number and, where relevant, tax identification number;
    
- for business accounts, documents relating to your business or registration; and
    
- information about the purpose of the account and the source of funds, where requested.
    

Some banks offer parts of the onboarding process remotely, while others require applicants to attend a branch in person.

## Practical tips

Bring more supporting documentation than you think you will need, and keep certified translations available if requested by the bank. During the compliance review, be prepared to explain the purpose of the account, your expected transactions and the origin of your funds.

Opening a bank account is always subject to the bank's internal compliance procedures and risk assessment, and approval cannot be guaranteed.
