The special visa — category **B** — is a short-term Georgian visa for semi-official travel that sits alongside the [diplomatic (A) visa](/en/diplomatic-visa-georgia). It is one of the five visa categories defined in Article 7 of the Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons. It is **not** used by tourists, relocators or remote workers; if that is you, see [visa-free entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia) or the [ordinary (C) visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia).

## The four subcategories

- **B1** — members of foreign delegations, or holders of business/official passports arriving on a working visit, together with accompanying family members and other accompanying persons.
- **B2** — administrative, technical and service personnel of foreign diplomatic missions, consular employees and service staff of consular offices, and their family members and private domestic servants; staff of international and humanitarian organisation offices in Georgia and their families; private domestic servants accompanying A2 diplomatic-visa holders.
- **B3** — individuals arriving under Georgia's bilateral and multilateral international treaties; members of peacekeeping forces and other military contingents deployed in Georgia under an international agreement, and their family members.
- **B4** — in the national interests of Georgia, individuals arriving on various missions.

## Type and validity

The special visa is a **short-term** visa. A single-entry short-term visa is issued for up to 30 days; multiple-entry short-term visas can carry validity of up to five years.

## Extension

Short-term special (and diplomatic) visas can be **extended** where the conditions for the respective visa are still met. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia receives the documentation and decides.

## Not the right category?

If your visit is ordinary rather than official, use [visa-free entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia) if your nationality is on the list, the [ordinary (C) visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia) for tourism, business or remote work, or an [immigration (D) visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia) if you are heading toward a residence permit.
