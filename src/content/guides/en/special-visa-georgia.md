The **special visa (category B)** is a **short-term Georgian visa** intended for certain official, governmental and international missions. It sits alongside the **[Diplomatic (A) Visa](/en/diplomatic-visa-georgia)** and is one of the visa categories established by **Article 7 of the Law of Georgia on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons**.

If you are travelling to Georgia for tourism, business, remote work or relocation, this is **not** the appropriate visa category. Instead, see **[Visa-Free Entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)**, the **[Ordinary (C) Visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia)** or the **[Immigration (D) Visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia)**.

## The four sub-categories

Georgia issues four types of special visas:

- **B1** — for members of official foreign delegations, holders of official or service passports travelling on a working visit, and their accompanying family members or other accompanying persons.
- **B2** — for administrative, technical and service staff of diplomatic missions and consular offices, their family members, private domestic staff, employees of international and humanitarian organisations operating in Georgia and their family members, as well as private domestic staff accompanying holders of an **A2 diplomatic visa**.
- **B3** — for persons travelling to Georgia under bilateral or multilateral international agreements, members of peacekeeping forces or other military contingents stationed in Georgia pursuant to an international treaty, and their accompanying family members.
- **B4** — for persons travelling to Georgia on various missions where their entry serves the national interests of Georgia.


## Type and validity

A special visa is a **short-term visa**.

It may be issued as:

- a **single-entry visa** permitting a stay of up to **30 calendar days**; or
- a **multiple-entry visa** with a validity period of **up to five years**, in accordance with the Law.

The type of visa issued depends on the purpose and circumstances of the visit.

## Extension

A special visa may be **extended** where the legal grounds for its issuance continue to exist.

Applications for an extension are submitted through the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia**, which considers the application and makes the relevant decision in accordance with the Law.

## Is this the right visa for you?

For almost all private travellers, the answer is **no**.

Most visitors to Georgia instead rely on:

- **[Visa-Free Entry](/en/visa-free-entry-georgia)**, if their nationality qualifies;
- the **[Ordinary (C) Visa](/en/ordinary-visa-georgia)** for tourism, private visits and most short-term business travel; or
- the **[Immigration (D) Visa](/en/immigration-visa-georgia)** for long-term purposes such as employment, study, family reunification or investment.
