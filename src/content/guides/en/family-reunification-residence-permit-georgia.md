The **Family Reunification Residence Permit** keeps families together when one member has a basis to live in Georgia. It lets the **family members of a foreigner who already holds a residence permit** — or of a **Georgian citizen** — obtain their own permit on the strength of that relationship. This guide explains who counts as family, the financial and documentary conditions, the renewal pattern and the fees, checked against the **Law on the Legal Status of Aliens and Stateless Persons** and **Government Resolution No. 520**.

## Who can apply

The permit is for the **family members** of:

a **foreigner who holds a valid Georgian residence permit** (the "sponsor"); or

a **Georgian citizen**.

In practice this covers a **spouse and minor children**, and other dependent family members recognised under the law. The sponsor's own status is the anchor — if it lapses, the reunification permit that depends on it is affected.

## Financial conditions

Because the permit is about supporting a family in Georgia, applicants generally must show **adequate means of support**. This is typically met by **monthly income of at least twice the subsistence minimum per family member**, or by holding **sufficient funds in a bank account**. Keep documentary proof — payslips, bank statements or similar — current as of the application.

## Proving the relationship

You evidence the family tie with **civil-status documents** — a **marriage certificate**, **birth certificate** and the like. Foreign documents must be **legalised or apostilled** unless covered by a treaty, and **translated into Georgian** where required. Pair these with the sponsor's **valid residence permit** or proof of **Georgian citizenship**.

## Duration and renewal

This is a **short-term** permit, generally issued for **six months to one year** and **renewable** while the qualifying relationship and conditions continue. Time spent lawfully on qualifying temporary permits can count toward the **ten years** required for a **[permanent residence permit](https://relocation.ge/en/permanent-residence-permit-georgia/)**; family members of Georgian citizens may also qualify for permanent residence by the family route directly.

## Fees and how to apply

Review fees depend on speed: **GEL 300** for a **30-day** decision, **GEL 450** for **20 days**, and **GEL 600** for **10 days**, plus a separate **residence-card** fee. Gather the relationship documents and proof of means, confirm the sponsor's status, and file **before any current permit expires**. Confirm current fees and the document list with the **Public Service Development Agency**.

This guide is general information, **not legal advice**. The list of qualifying family members, the financial test and fees can change, so confirm the current position with the **Public Service Development Agency** or an adviser before relying on a particular treatment.
