This guide explains the process of **importing a vehicle into Georgia**—including customs clearance and registration—as well as **buying a vehicle that is already registered in Georgia**. For information about driving licences, foreign-registered vehicles and compulsory insurance, see our **[Driving in Georgia](/en/driving-in-georgia)** guide.

This guide provides general information only. Before importing a vehicle, always confirm the current legal requirements, taxes and administrative procedures with the competent authorities.

Two authorities are primarily involved:

- the **Revenue Service of Georgia**, which administers **customs procedures and import taxes**; and
- the **Service Agency of the Ministry of Internal Affairs**, which is responsible for **vehicle registration and licence plates**.

## Step 1 — Customs clearance and import taxes

If you bring a vehicle into Georgia for permanent use, it must first be **cleared through customs** with the **Revenue Service**.

Depending on the vehicle and the applicable customs rules, importation may be subject to one or more taxes, including:

- **excise tax**, which is generally determined by factors such as the vehicle's **engine capacity and age**;
- **VAT**, where applicable under Georgian tax legislation; and
- any other customs charges applicable to the particular vehicle.

The taxes payable depend on the vehicle's characteristics, customs value and the applicable legislation. Before importing a vehicle, always confirm the current taxes and customs procedures with the **Revenue Service**, and rely on the official customs assessment rather than unofficial online estimates.

## How excise works

Excise on passenger cars is generally calculated **per cubic centimetre (cm³) of engine displacement**, with the applicable rate depending on the vehicle's **age**.

Under the **2026 amendments to the Tax Code of Georgia**:

|Vehicle age|Basic excise rate|
|---|--:|
|0 to 6 years (inclusive)|**GEL 1.5 per cm³**|
|More than 6 years|**GEL 4.5 per cm³**|

Special rules apply to certain vehicles:

- **Left-hand-drive hybrid vehicles** benefit from a preferential excise rate reduced by **60%** of the applicable basic rate.
- **Right-hand-drive vehicles** are generally subject to **three times** the applicable basic excise rate.

For customs purposes, a vehicle's **age** is generally determined by comparing the **year in which the customs declaration is registered** with the vehicle's **year of manufacture**.

**Electric vehicles** are subject to separate excise provisions under the Tax Code. Because their treatment differs from vehicles with an internal combustion engine, always confirm the current excise applicable to your vehicle with the **Revenue Service**.

The 2026 amendments also reduced the age threshold for **classic vehicles** from **40 years** to **more than 30 years** and introduced transitional provisions for certain vehicles that had already been imported or were in transit when the amendments entered into force. As excise rules and transitional arrangements may change over time, always confirm the current rates and their application with the **Revenue Service** before importing a vehicle.

## Step 2 — Register the vehicle and obtain Georgian licence plates

Once the vehicle has been cleared through customs, it must be registered with the **Service Agency of the Ministry of Internal Affairs**, which is responsible for vehicle registration and the issue of Georgian licence plates.

Registration is carried out at any Service Agency registration office, regardless of your home or registered address in Georgia.

### Registration deadlines

Different statutory deadlines apply depending on the circumstances:

- a vehicle imported **for permanent registration in Georgia** must generally be registered **within 60 days**; and
    
- where a foreign vehicle remains in Georgia **for more than 90 days**, temporary registration requirements may apply.
    

As the applicable deadline depends on the customs procedure and the vehicle's status, always confirm the current requirements with the **Service Agency** and the **Revenue Service**.

### Documents for first registration

You will generally need:

- a completed **registration application**;
- a valid **identity document**;
- a document proving **ownership** of the vehicle;
- a **vehicle inspection report (დათვალიერების აქტი)** issued as part of the registration process;
- proof of payment of the applicable **state fees**; and
- **internal transit documents**, where required.

### Registration fees

|Item|Fee|
|---|--:|
|Vehicle inspection report (დათვალიერების აქტი)|**GEL 20**|
|Georgian licence plates|**GEL 80**|
|Vehicle registration certificate|**GEL 100**|

Following registration, the Service Agency records the vehicle's principal details in the state register, including its make, model, body type, **vehicle identification number (VIN)**, year of manufacture, colour, engine number, engine displacement, fuel type, permitted and unladen weight, and any registered legal encumbrances.

The Agency then issues the **Georgian licence plates** and the **vehicle registration certificate**.

## Buying a vehicle already registered in Georgia

If you buy a vehicle that is **already registered in Georgia**, no customs import procedure is required. Instead, ownership is transferred through a **re-registration procedure** at the **Service Agency of the Ministry of Internal Affairs**. Re-registration is required whenever ownership changes through a sale, gift or exchange.

### Documents for re-registration

You will generally need:

- a document proving **ownership** of the vehicle;
- valid **identity document(s)** of the parties;
- a **vehicle inspection report (დათვალიერების აქტი)**;
- a completed **joint application** signed by the buyer and seller; and
- proof of payment of the applicable **state fees**.

### Re-registration fees

|Item|Fee|
|---|--:|
|Vehicle inspection report (დათვალიერების აქტი)|**GEL 20**|
|Georgian licence plates (where issued)|**GEL 80**|
|Vehicle registration certificate|**GEL 100**|

Once the re-registration is completed, the vehicle is recorded in the buyer's name in the state register and a new **vehicle registration certificate** is issued.

## Before you import or buy

Before importing or purchasing a vehicle in Georgia:

- obtain the **customs valuation** and estimated import taxes for your specific vehicle from the **Revenue Service**before beginning the customs clearance procedure;
- remember that **excise tax** is generally calculated based on the vehicle's **engine displacement** and **age**, with special rules applying to certain hybrid and right-hand-drive vehicles;
- budget for the applicable **registration fees** charged by the **Service Agency**, in addition to any customs duties or taxes that may apply; and
- if purchasing a vehicle already registered in Georgia, verify the vehicle's registration details and check that there are no registered legal encumbrances before completing the transaction.

For information about driving licences, foreign-registered vehicles and compulsory motor insurance, see our **[Driving in Georgia](/en/driving-in-georgia)** guide.
